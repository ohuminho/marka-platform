# MARKA, Asset-Light Platform Operating Model

## Strategic position

MARKA is a digital marketplace, intermediary and services platform. It connects customers with independent sellers, service providers, drivers, transport operators, property owners, insurers, event organizers and other verified partners.

MARKA does not need to own the physical assets listed or used by these partners. The default model is partner-owned inventory, vehicles, property, equipment, venues and operational capacity. MARKA supplies discovery, digital tools, matching, communication, trust controls and, where legally and technically approved, transaction coordination.

MARKA CV and similar digital utilities are MARKA-provided software services; they do not change the asset-light operating model.

## Asset-light principles

- No MARKA-owned delivery fleet, taxi fleet, warehouse network, retail inventory, event venue, insurance risk book or solar installation inventory as a prerequisite for the platform.
- Partners remain responsible for the assets, service delivery, permits, insurance, warranties, product accuracy, safety and applicable professional qualifications within their legal obligations.
- MARKA verifies partner identity and relevant evidence proportionately; verification must not be represented as a guarantee of service quality, solvency or regulatory approval beyond the checks actually completed.
- Partner listings must identify the responsible seller/provider and disclose relevant fees, conditions, cancellation, returns, warranties and service limits.
- The platform must support partner onboarding, verification, ratings, complaints, dispute evidence and suspension controls.

## Platform responsibilities

MARKA is responsible for its own software, account security, data protection, search and discovery, platform moderation, transaction records, transparent platform fees, support channels and the controls it explicitly offers.

The asset-light model does not remove MARKA's own legal or consumer-protection duties. Each vertical must undergo an Angola-specific legal and operational review before launch, especially where intermediation, transport, insurance distribution, ticketing, property, financial services or personal data are regulated.

## Vertical boundaries

| Vertical | Partner responsibility | MARKA role |
|---|---|---|
| Marketplace | Product ownership, accuracy, stock, delivery/returns and warranty | Listing, discovery, seller trust, order coordination |
| Mobility and delivery | Vehicle, driver, permits, safety and fulfilment | Matching, routing, fare/fee calculation, dispatch and trip records |
| Cargo and transport | Vehicle capacity, cargo handling, permits and delivery | Request discovery, matching, quote and job coordination |
| Property | Ownership/authority, property details and lawful transaction | Listings, enquiries, viewing coordination and partner verification |
| Insurance | Licensed insurer/intermediary, underwriting, policy terms and claims | Discovery and only legally permitted referral/intermediation |
| Events and tickets | Organizer, event, venue, capacity, admission and refunds | Event discovery, ticket technology, anti-counterfeit checks |
| Solar and energy | Equipment, installation, technical design, warranty and maintenance | Catalog, supplier discovery and quote requests |
| MARKA CV | User-provided facts and final review of CV content | CV editor, templates, watermarking, PDF export and optional AI assistance |

## Revenue model

Use revenue streams that do not require owning the underlying physical assets:
- disclosed commission or referral fees where legally permitted and contractually agreed;
- seller/provider subscriptions and verification services, with basic discovery kept accessible;
- promoted listings and advertising clearly labeled as sponsored;
- transparent platform/service fees;
- digital product purchases, including MARKA CV Premium exports or template packages.

Do not charge for verification in a way that implies a paid badge guarantees regulatory approval or business quality. Do not make basic access conditional on purchasing promotion.

## Payments and custody

- Prefer direct settlement to the responsible seller/provider or an approved payment split/disbursement arrangement.
- MARKA must not describe itself as holding partner or customer funds in a wallet unless a separately approved and legally compliant custody/payment model exists.
- Record platform fees and partner proceeds distinctly.
- Payment confirmation must be authoritative and server-side; client redirects and screenshots are not proof.
- Do not enable live collection, escrow, stored value, payouts or split settlement until the payment provider contract, regulatory role, reconciliation, refunds, chargebacks and sandbox/end-to-end tests are validated.

## Shared platform capabilities

Build shared capabilities once and reuse them across verticals:
- MARKA ID and role-based access;
- partner onboarding and verification;
- search, listings and category taxonomy;
- messaging and notifications;
- ratings, complaints, moderation and audit trail;
- quote, booking and order primitives where appropriate;
- payment orchestration only after approval;
- analytics and fraud controls with privacy safeguards.

Do not force every vertical into the same Product, Order or Booking data model when its lifecycle differs. Use domain-specific records connected to shared identity, audit and payment references.

## Rollout discipline

1. Make each vertical discoverable without hiding existing services.
2. Validate partner supply and customer demand before adding operational complexity.
3. Launch low-risk lead/listing flows before regulated or money-moving workflows.
4. Add booking, dispatch or ticket lifecycle only with server-side state transitions and tests.
5. Release payments only after partner, legal, provider and reconciliation readiness.
6. Measure contribution margin per vertical, including support, fraud, refunds, payment fees and infrastructure cost.

## Non-goals

The platform must not imply that MARKA owns, operates or guarantees partner assets or services where it does not. Do not create physical fleet, inventory, warehouse or venue operations merely to make a vertical appear complete. Do not use "middleman" as a substitute for defining MARKA's actual legal role in each regulated market.
